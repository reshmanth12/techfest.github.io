About TechFest

TechFest 2026 is a national-level college event organized for students interested in coding, robotics, gaming, AI, and innovation.

Registrations are now open for all departments!

Main Events

  • Hackathon Competition
  • Web Development Contest
  • Gaming Tournament
  • Robotics Exhibition
  • Quiz Competition

Participation Process

  1. Fill registration form
  2. Select event category
  3. Pay registration fee
  4. Receive confirmation email
  5. Attend the event

Event Definitions

Hackathon
A 24-hour coding competition where teams solve real-world problems.
Robotics
Students showcase automated machines and robot projects.
Gaming Tournament
Multiplayer gaming competition for students.
